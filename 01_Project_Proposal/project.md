# OceanGuard – Project Proposal

## Title
OceanGuard – Cleaner Seas, Healthier Future

## Problem statement
Sea pollution from plastic, fishing waste, sewage, oil and other materials affects coastal environments, marine life and communities. Pollution observations may be scattered, making it difficult to understand where problems occur and which categories need attention.

## Proposed solution
OceanGuard is a proposed sea-pollution monitoring and awareness platform. It brings pollution reporting, location mapping, waste classification, analytics, hotspot identification, awareness and cleanup activities together.

## Objectives
- Organize observations of pollution by type and location.
- Clean and analyze the collected dataset.
- Identify common pollution categories and patterns over time or place where fields allow.
- Present findings through clear charts and an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Communicate data limitations and encourage awareness and cleanup.

## Main features
Pollution reporting, optional photo evidence, pollution map, waste classification, hotspot analysis, awareness content and cleanup campaigns.

## Target users
Primary: students, beach visitors, fishermen and local communities. Secondary: environmental organizations, NGOs, government/local authorities, researchers and coastal tourism businesses.

## Questions to investigate
1. Which pollution category is most common in the supplied dataset?
2. How do recorded pollution indicators vary by country or city, if location fields are present?
3. How do observations vary over time, if a valid date field is present?
4. Which locations have the highest recorded pollution index?
5. Which pollution categories should be prioritized for awareness and cleanup?

## Expected deliverables
Proposal, documented source dataset, cleaned CSV, cleaning script, analysis outputs, visualizations and Power BI dashboard.

## Scope and limitations
Findings depend on the supplied dataset's coverage, definitions and quality. The dashboard is an academic visualization, not a live ocean-monitoring service. Do not claim real-time monitoring or verified environmental measurements unless supported by the source.
